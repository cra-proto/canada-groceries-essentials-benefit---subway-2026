How much you can get

The Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit payments are not taxable and credit amounts will vary based on your situation. You do not have to report the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit payments you receive on your tax return.

  • Where to find your benefit amount

How your benefit is calculated

Your Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit payments are based on the following:

  • Your adjusted family net income
  • Your marital status
  • The number of eligible children under 19 years old that you have registered for the Canada child benefit, Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit, or both

For details: Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit calculation sheets

Payment amounts are recalculated every July

Your Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit amount is recalculated every July based on the information from your previous year's tax return. Each Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit payment period starts in July and ends in June of the following year.

For example, the information from your 2024 tax return determines the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit amount you get for the payment period from July 2025 to June 2026. You could get up to:

  • $679 if you are a single individual
  • $890 if you are married or have a common-law partner
  • $234 for each eligible child under the age of 19

For details: GST/HST credit payment amounts

An increase of your 2024 adjusted family net income could affect your payments starting in July 2025.

Find out the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit amount you may be entitled to: Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit payments chart

What affects your payment calculation

The Canada Revenue Agency recalculates your benefit payment when:

  • A reassessment of either your or your spouse or common-law partner's tax return results in a change to your adjusted family net income
  • Your eligible child turns 19 years of age
  • Your marital status changes
  • The number of eligible children in your care changes
  • A recipient dies
  • You start, or stop, sharing custody of a child

Be sure to keep your personal information up to date to avoid any disruption to your payments.

Where to find your benefit amount

If you are entitled to the benefit, the CRA will send you a Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit notice based on the information from your assessed tax return. The notice provides your annual entitlement along with the payment schedule for your quarterly payments.

If your adjusted family net income is too high, the CRA will not send you a notice since you are not entitled to receive the credit.

If you do not have a CRA account, the CRA will send your notice by mail.

If you have a CRA account

You can sign in to your CRA account to view:

  • Your next expected payment date and amount
  • The status and amount of each payment for the year, from July to the following June
  • Your statement of account for the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit, including details about amounts and entitlements, dates, and balances

  3. Select Benefits and credits to find detailed payment information

Adjusted family net income

If you have a spouse or common-law partner, your adjusted net incomes are combined to get your adjusted family net income (AFNI).

Your AFNI is:

Your family net income (line 23600 of your tax return, plus line 23600 of your spouse's or common-law partner's tax return, if applicable)
−
Minus any Universal Child Care Benefit (UCCB) and registered disability savings plan (RDSP) income received (line 11700 and line 12500 of your or your spouse's tax return, if applicable)
+
Plus any UCCB and RDSP amounts repaid (line 21300 and line 23200 of your or your spouse's tax return, if applicable)
